Hello!

I am a Games Programmer studying Computer Games Technology at Abertay University in my 4th and final year of study.
I have a keen love for games, and game development, across a whole host of areas, and especially in the creation of game systems, tools and engines that can help make artistic visions into reality.
Using my skills in programming, mathematics, and problem solving, as well as my dedication and tenacity, I want to make games, and help teams get games made!

Outside of game development, I enjoy photography, and im an avid fan of a lot of different types of media, from films to tabletop games like Warhammer - but chief amongst them, I am a musician, spending my free time playing banjo, bass, or guitar.
